Jetball privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Jetball is made by Uniq Games, the studio of Jan Bartos, a sole trader registered in the Czech Republic (IČO 21978841, Horni Lucni 17, Breclav, Czech Republic) ("we"). This policy explains what information Jetball uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: uniqgames.support@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your headset

Jetball saves these on your headset only. We can't see them:

  • your settings (volume, graphics, comfort, voice);
  • your training and solo progress, your solo rating and the currency you earn in the game;
  • your Locker choices and the items you own;
  • the pilot name you pick, and the players you block;
  • a random player ID the game creates. It is not your Meta account and is not linked to your identity.

Uninstalling Jetball deletes them. You can export your solo progress from Settings › Solo progress.

2. What leaves your headset

When you play online:

  • To put you in a match and run it, the game sends your pilot name, your random player ID and your in-game actions (movement, throws, shots) to our match servers and to Photon (Exit Games GmbH, Hamburg, Germany), which connects players.
  • Your IP address is used to make the connection, as with any online service.
  • Other players in the match see your pilot name and your cosmetic choices.
  • Our match servers keep logs for up to 30 days to fix problems and handle reports.

Team voice in online matches:

  • If voice is on, the game uses your headset's microphone (your Quest asks for permission first) and sends what you say, live, to the players on your team in that match, through Photon Voice (Exit Games GmbH). The other team never receives it.
  • Voice is not recorded or stored, by us or on our servers. It is passed on live and is gone when the match ends.
  • You can turn your microphone off (MIC) or all voice off (VOICE) in the online menu, and mute or block any player.

Ranked leaderboards:

  • When you finish a Ranked match, your Ranked rating and pilot name are saved on Meta's leaderboard service for Jetball, linked to your Meta account. Other Jetball players can see your pilot name, rating and rank on the leaderboard.
  • To remove your entry, write to uniqgames.support@gmail.com.

Inviting friends:

  • When you're in a private room, Jetball tells Meta which room you're in (Meta calls this group presence), so friends you invite with INVITE FRIENDS can join you. Meta shows the invite screen and your friends; Jetball never receives your friends list. This stops when you leave the room.

Purchases:

  • Some cosmetic items (pilots, jet finishes, exhaust colours) can be bought as add-ons in the Shop. Meta handles the payment in its own checkout; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Jetball asks Meta which add-ons your Meta account owns and what they cost in your currency, only to show prices and unlock what you bought. The list of add-ons you own is also kept on your headset, so they work offline.
  • We don't send purchase information to our servers. Refunds and purchase history are handled by Meta.

Engine statistics: the Unity engine that Jetball is built with sends anonymous technical statistics about your device to Unity Technologies (for example the device model and graphics capabilities).

Crash reports: if Jetball crashes, Meta's system may collect a crash report, which we can see in our developer dashboard. It contains technical information, not your name. Meta's own handling of your data is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't record or store voice chat.

4. Other players and safety

Pilot names are checked against a list of words that aren't allowed. There is no text chat. On the PLAYERS page of the online menu you can mute or block any player (a block is remembered on your headset; they aren't told) and report them: a report sends their pilot name, the reason and the match time to our match server. You can also write to uniqgames.support@gmail.com with their pilot name and the time of the match. We check our server logs and can remove a name or block a player from online play.

5. Children

Jetball is intended for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used our online features, contact us and we will delete their information.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or give you a copy. Write to uniqgames.support@gmail.com with your pilot name and, if you can, the time of your last online match. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ, uoou.gov.cz), or the authority where you live.

Legal basis: to provide the game and the online play you ask for, performance of our agreement with you; to keep online play fair and safe and to fix problems, our legitimate interests.

7. Security and storage

We protect what we store with access controls and encrypted connections. Our match servers run in Switzerland, and Photon connects players through its servers in the European Union.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.

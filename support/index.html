Report a problem

  • In the game: Settings or the pause menu › REPORT A PROBLEM shows our email and a QR code. Scanning it opens an email with your build number filled in.
  • Beta testers: use the feedback option on JetGoal's page in your Quest library or the Meta Horizon app. Short video recordings help us most.
  • Please tell us what you were doing, what you expected, what happened, and the build number (bottom of the Settings page).

Report a player

During an online match, open the menu (left menu button) › PLAYERS › REPORT, or email us with their pilot name and the time of the match. You can also MUTE or BLOCK them on the same page.
